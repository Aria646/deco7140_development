Visceral Design

Visceral design emphasizes the immediate emotional reaction users have when they first encounter a platform. By leveraging visual elements such as color, typography, and layout, it aims to evoke an instinctive attraction and establish a strong initial appeal for the recipe search and community website.

Page Layouts

These wireframes illustrate the comprehensive structure of the recipe and community website, highlighting clear navigation, functional layouts, and visually balanced content organization. The homepage features a hero banner with an introduction and "Get Started" button, guiding users to explore recipes and community content. The food news and forum pages focus on dynamic content, showcasing the latest updates, trending posts, and featured discussions in grid layouts. The recipes and ingredients pages provide categorized recipe displays, ingredient details, and action buttons for user interaction. The about us page presents team introductions, company background, and a contact section with a form and map for accessibility. The mobile versions adapt these layouts into a single-column format, ensuring smooth navigation and readability on smaller screens. Together, these wireframes demonstrate a cohesive design system that emphasizes clarity, usability, and community engagement.

The reason for choosing Warm Tomato Red (#E74C3C) as the primary color is that it evokes strong sensory appeal and emotional warmth. Psychologically, red is closely associated with food, passion, and vitality, making it an ideal color to stimulate appetite and draw users' attention. Its high visibility allows it to highlight key buttons, icons, or promotional areas, ensuring important content stands out instantly. Soft Apricot Orange (#F5A623) is chosen as the secondary color because it conveys warmth, friendliness, and optimism. This color supports a welcoming and energetic community atmosphere, encouraging user engagement and participation. It complements the primary red beautifully, adding depth and visual harmony to the overall design. Dark Cocoa Brown (#3E2723)serves as the text color, providing excellent contrast against the light background. It maintains readability and eye comfort, while giving the website a natural, grounded, and trustworthy tone—ideal for long-form recipe reading or discussions. Light Cream Beige (#FAF3E0) is selected as the background color to create a cozy and inviting visual environment reminiscent of a home kitchen. Its soft tone reduces eye strain and balances the intensity of the warmer hues, ensuring a pleasant browsing experience. Together, this color palette builds a warm, appetizing, and community-oriented atmosphere, allowing users to feel both energized and comfortable as they explore recipes, share experiences, and connect with others.

Typography

Typography choices are essential in establishing a friendly and approachable visual tone for the website. The “Delius” font family is selected for its handwritten yet legible style, evoking a sense of warmth, creativity, and calmness. Its smooth curves and consistent stroke weight create a welcoming atmosphere while maintaining clear readability on both desktop and mobile screens.

Font sizes and weights are carefully structured to ensure visual hierarchy and comfort. Larger headings draw attention to key sections, while smaller body text supports easy reading and flow. This typographic setup enhances the site's personality — informal, friendly, and accessible — aligning with the community-driven and creative nature of the platform.

  • H1: 4.25rem, bold (Page titles)
  • H2: 3rem, semi-bold (Section headings)
  • H3: 2rem, medium (Sub-sections)
  • Paragraph: 1.25rem, normal (Body text)
  • Caption: 1.25rem, light

The "Delius" typeface balances character and readability. Its rounded strokes and generous spacing make it suitable for storytelling, recipes, and community posts — delivering a human touch that enhances emotional connection and user engagement.
