Michelin Announces Its New Starred Restaurant List

October 26, 2024 · By Editorial Desk

The 2024 Michelin Guide has officially been released, bringing a mix of expected promotions and a few headline-making surprises. This year's list shows that Michelin is paying closer attention to restaurants that combine technical skill with a strong sense of place, sustainable sourcing, and an approachable dining experience.

Several restaurants earned their very first star for menus built around seasonal and local ingredients — especially coastal produce, heritage vegetables, and modern takes on regional comfort dishes. At the same time, a number of long-standing one-star venues were promoted after demonstrating steady quality and a clearer concept over multiple years.

Sustainability continues to be part of the inspectors' evaluation. Venues that could show transparent supply chains, reduced-waste practices, or close partnerships with nearby farms were clearly favoured. For some restaurants, this focus was the deciding factor between receiving a Bib Gourmand and joining the one-star category.

Another important shift is geography. Instead of spotlighting only capital-city fine dining, Michelin expanded its attention to suburban and coastal kitchens where chef-owners cook in open, energetic rooms — similar to the scene in the image above. This aligns with what diners have wanted in 2024: high-level food served in warmer, more informal spaces.

Of course, the guide also brought its share of pressure. Newly starred restaurants will now face full bookings and bigger expectations, while a small number of demotions were linked to inconsistency in service or loss of menu focus. Michelin is making it clear that recognition is an ongoing standard, not a one-time prize.

Overall, the 2024 list signals that Michelin is rewarding cooking that is precise but human — food that respects ingredients, tells a local story, and still feels welcoming to guests. Diners can expect less showy theatricality and more kitchens where chefs are visibly at work, delivering dishes with personality.
