Japanese Egg Rice Set

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 15 min · Serves 1

Ingredients

  • Warm steamed rice, 1 bowl
  • 2 eggs (for a fluffy omelette)
  • 1 bowl miso soup (with tofu and spring onion)
  • 1 small portion of mixed beans or side dish
  • Black sesame, soy sauce to taste

Directions

  1. Cook the rice first and keep it warm.
  2. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt, then pan-fry on low heat to make a soft omelette.
  3. Plate: put the rice in a bowl, place the omelette on top and sprinkle black sesame.
  4. Serve with hot miso soup and the bean/side dish.

Tips

To match the warm, painterly look of the image, you can add a little carrot, pumpkin, or yellow miso to bring out warmer tones.
